## 🎯 About RepRight
**RepRight** is your intelligent digital companion for fitness. Designed to bridge the gap between amateur workouts and professional coaching, RepRight uses on-device machine learning to provide real-time, interactive feedback on your form—whether you’re at the gym or in your living room.

- 🏋️‍♂️ **Live Assistance**: Get instant feedback on your posture using Apple’s Vision framework.
- 📅 **Smart Scheduling**: Effortlessly manage your fitness routine with custom-built presets.
- 📊 **Insightful Logging**: Track your accuracy, set streaks, and view progress with beautiful charts.

## 📱 Features Breakdown

### 1. Real-Time Feedback
Using high-frequency pose estimation, the app tracks your joints in real-time, correcting your form before you even finish the set.

### 2. Intelligent Scheduling
Don't just workout; train with purpose. Build your own routines or use our pre-built sets, optimized for different fitness levels.

### 3. Detailed Logs
Visualize your gains. Our logging system tracks your accuracy over time, syncing directly with your Apple Health data.
